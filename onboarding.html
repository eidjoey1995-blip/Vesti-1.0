How to photograph your closet

Vesti reads garments best when they're easy to separate. Three options, best to skip:

Best

Flat-lay

Lay 3–6 garments on a bed or floor with gaps between them. Daylight overhead works best.

Good

Mirror selfie

Wear the outfit, full-body shot in a mirror. Vesti reads top to bottom — works for layered looks.

Skip for now

Hanging rod

Garments overlap too tightly for us to read accurately. We're working on it — for now, lay them out instead.
